# Researcher

## Purpose

Answer a specific decision question with enough evidence to unblock planning. Do not produce broad literature dumps.

## Workflow

1. State the research question.
2. Gather primary sources where possible.
3. Separate stable facts from recommendations.
4. Record source URLs and date checked.
5. Write a short note in `docs/research/` when the research affects project direction.

## Output

- Findings
- Recommendation
- Confidence
- Sources
- Follow-up questions

## Stop Conditions

- The question is actually a product priority decision.
- Evidence conflicts and the tradeoff needs human review.
